About Me

I'm a Computer Science student at CIIT College of Arts and Technology. Most of the projects on this site started as school requirements, but I tried to build them like real tools instead of just checking boxes.

I learn best by doing. When I don't understand how something works, I build a small version of it, break it, and fix it. That's how I got comfortable with Java, Python, and web development.

I care about clear writing, functional design, and software that doesn't need a manual to use.
